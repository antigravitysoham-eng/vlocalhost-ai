## The tagline

**Your Voice Hosted Locally.**

It carries the name — everything happens on localhost — and states the
mechanism in four words. Used on every banner, both variants; the Independence
Day nod is carried by the tricolour trace rather than by a second line.

## The one-liners, for reuse

- Meeting notes that never leave your machine.
- No bot joins your call. Nothing leaves your laptop.
- Your voice stays yours.
- Made in India, runs on your machine, costs nothing.
- On-device by construction — there is no endpoint to switch off.

## Words to avoid

**"Revolutionary", "game-changing", "AI-powered"** — every competitor says
them. **"Enterprise-grade", "SOC 2", "bank-level security"** — not true and not
needed. **"Private by design"** on its own — say the mechanism instead: nothing
is uploaded because nothing has anywhere to upload to.
